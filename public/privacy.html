1. Who we are

TinderAI is an independent AI conversation app operated by Pravat Kumar Mishra. This policy covers information handled through TinderAI. For privacy questions or requests, contact me.sanuvai@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

We store your account ID, email address, account creation time, and a password hash if you register with a password. Your profile can contain your first and last name, age, ethnicity, gender, biography, personality type, and photo. We also store your matches and conversation history, including messages, AI replies, authors, and timestamps. Some profile fields can reveal sensitive information; share only what you are comfortable providing.

3. Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in or link Google to your account, we receive your Google account identifier, verified email, and available profile information such as your name and picture. We use Google credentials to verify your identity. We do not receive your Google password. Google also handles information under its Privacy Policy.

4. How we use information

We use account details to authenticate you and maintain your session; profile details to personalize the experience; photos to display your profile; and matches and messages to provide conversations. We also process information as needed to maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and respond to requests. We do not sell your personal information or use the app’s data for targeted advertising.

5. AI processing and service providers

When you send a message, the backend sends the conversation history and profile context to its configured AI model provider to generate a reply. The profile context includes your name, age, ethnicity, and gender, together with the AI persona’s details. The provider depends on the deployment configuration; contact us for the current provider and its handling of prompts. Do not assume prompts are excluded from provider retention or training. Hosting and database services process stored account and chat data. Cloudflare R2 stores newly uploaded profile photos, and Pollinations is used to generate AI persona images. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

6. Photo visibility and sharing

New profile photos are stored at publicly accessible URLs. Anyone who obtains a photo URL can view it without signing in, and copies or caches may exist outside our control. Matches and conversation endpoints are restricted to the owning account. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service, users, or others from abuse or security threats.

7. Cookies and browser storage

An HttpOnly refresh cookie keeps you signed in; its default lifetime is seven days, though server configuration can change it. Access tokens are held in browser memory rather than persistent browser storage. Local storage remembers your light, dark, or system appearance preference. The app does not implement advertising or analytics trackers. Google sign-in and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs under their own policies. Blocking cookies may prevent sign-in from working.

8. Retention and security

Account records, profiles, matches, and chat history currently remain stored until manually removed; the app has no automatic deletion schedule for them. Refresh session records expire according to the configured session lifetime. Replacing a photo does not automatically remove the old stored image. Contact us to request removal, including old photos. Provider logs, backups, and caches may have separate retention periods; contact us for details. We use password hashing and authenticated access controls, but no service can guarantee complete security.

9. Your choices and requests

You can update your profile from your account page, change your appearance preference, sign out, or stop using the app. For access, correction, a copy of your data, or account and data deletion, email me.sanuvai@gmail.com from your account email when possible. Do not send your password. We may need to verify ownership before acting. There is currently no self-service export or deletion feature. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to your local data protection authority. Requests and any necessary retention are handled subject to applicable law.

10. Adults only

TinderAI is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange removal where appropriate.

11. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when features or data practices change. The date above shows the current version, and material changes will be identified on this page. Contact me.sanuvai@gmail.com with any questions about this policy or the current deployment’s providers, retention, or international processing.
